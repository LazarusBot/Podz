# Podz design

The source of truth is the Paper file **Podz** (frames: Onboarding 1-6, Home,
Home2, DetailView, each in dark and white). This file records what those
frames use so the app can follow them without opening Paper.

**Every UI change must follow this file, and must update it when it adds a
color, a button style or a pattern.** Check it before every commit/push.

## Principles

- **Words.** The app says **hut** in English (**refuge** in French), never pod or shelter ("Refuges" and "Cabins" stay as the two kinds of hut on Home): "3 huts found", "View hut", "No huts match". **Podz** is the name of the app and nothing else (pod stays the name of the code's type and the database table). **Lenny is "he"** everywhere ("Tell him where and when. He finds you a bed."); "it" is only ever the thing he finds.

- **UX comes first.** Ease of use beats visual polish and beats adding
  features. Before building, check how many gestures and controls share one
  screen: horizontal paging, vertical swipes, edge swipes, taps on the same
  area and system gestures (swipe-to-dismiss, keyboard dismiss) all compete.
  If a screen is getting crowded or two interactions could fight (the pod
  detail's photo paging vs. swipe-to-dismiss is the example), **tell the
  user before building** and propose the simpler option; remove the
  interaction that is hardest to get right.
- **The traveler app has three tabs: Home, Likes, Profile** (`TravelerTab`). The old Pods tab (friends' posts, the offline Trip Pass) and Posts are gone for now: Posts is a later feature. (The host side, with its tabs, mailbox and Add-a-pod form, was removed from the app; it is in git history.)
- Pure black / white grounds. **No accent color**: the photos carry all the color.
- Flat. No shadows, no gradients except a fade into the ground and the soft black scrim under a name on a photo tile (white text on photos is the one place text isn't a token). Nothing is glass:
  the Likes folders are flat bubbles (see Patterns).
- Dark and light are both designed. Never hard-code a color: use a token below,
  built with `Color(light:dark:)`.
- Quiet copy: short, lower-key sentences ("Tell him where and when.").

## Shape and spacing

- Content margin 24. Button rows sit 40 from the screen edges on the pod detail, 24 (the grid's edge) on the Likes folder, 56 on onboarding.
- Pill buttons: capsule, **83 tall**, 13 apart. Search pill: 60 tall. Tab bar: 220 x 64.
- Pod cards: 296 x 340, radius 36 continuous, caption 12 below. A peek of the next card (card width = screen - 97).
- Photos that bleed from the top: rounded only along the bottom, radius 75. Onboarding card photo: radius 53, inset 21.
- Lenny mark: 40 round in the search pill; 30 on a `LennyReply` bubble. The pod detail has no Lenny badge on the photo any more: its pager dots are plain (8, white). (Details in the **Pod detail** bullet below.)
- **Control stack** (the row of pills at the bottom of the pod detail, the Likes folder, the save-to-folder sheet and every page Profile opens: back pill + the page's action): the pills are 83 tall, 13 apart, and the row sits **36 from the screen's physical bottom edge** on every screen, i.e. 2 above the 34pt home-indicator zone: `DetailLayout.controlStackGap` (36 − 34 = 2) plus the inset. One rule: a screen that ignores the bottom safe area (a tab's content, the pod detail) uses `.controlStackPadding()`; a sheet, which already respects it, uses `DetailLayout.controlStackGap` alone. Never hard-code another number. Side margins: 24 on the Likes folder, the save sheet and Profile's pages; 40 on the pod detail, **46 in the pod detail's card layout** (the booking questions and "Did you book it?"), so the pills, the picker included, **and the answer chips under the card** ("2 guests") share the card's edges (`BookingChips(inset:)`); Lenny's bubble keeps its 24. **Taps are one rule for every pill** (`DetailRoundButton`, `DetailTextButton`, the picker's round buttons): the whole pill is the hit target (`contentShape` of its own shape, not just the glyph or the label), a pill never swaps for a different view when its state changes (the booking stack's right-hand pill is one button whose words and fill fade, so a tap landing mid-change still reaches it), a pill that can't act yet is dimmed and disabled (the back pill while it books) instead of taking a tap that does nothing, the press answers at once (scale 0.97 in 0.08 s, back in `AppMotion.snappy`), and the haptic generator is built at launch (`TapScaleButtonStyle.warmUp()`) so the first tap isn't slower than the rest.

## Buttons

All buttons are flat capsules in `homePill` (95% opacity) with a 1pt ground-colored border, and use `TapScaleButtonStyle` for the press.

- **Text pill** ("Book", "Open", "Continue", "Touch to start"): 14 regular label in `homeText`.
- **Highlighted pill** (last onboarding step): ground and text swap, `onbHighlight` fill, 14 semiBold label.
- **Back**: 83 x 83 pill, chevron 8 x 12, 1.5 stroke, round caps, `homeText` at 35%.
- **Caption tap area.** The small 13 `homeSubtitle` captions ("Details ›", "See all", "Another tip ›", Lenny's "Go to Home ›", "Find huts ›"...) look and sit exactly as drawn but take taps in a band about 44 tall: `.captionTapArea()` grows the tappable shape (not the layout) by 8 on every side, 10 for a caption with 4 of padding, 14 for one with none. Use it on every new caption; never pad one taller to get there, it would move the stack.
- **Close** (`CloseButton`, 44): round pill, same fill and 1pt border as back, thin 12pt "x" (1.5 stroke, round caps) in `homeText` at 45%. **In a control stack (83 round, beside the back pill) the "x" is 9 x 9 at 35%**, the same height and weight as the back chevron's ink (9 tall, 35%), so the two pills read as a pair. The add-friend and share-folder sheets use it; it is the only close button (the old gray circle is gone).
- **Share** (Likes folder): round 83 pill like Back, the app's SF Symbol `link` (16 semibold) in `homeText` at 35%. Not the vector from the Paper example, which wasn't right.
- **Like**: 83 x 83 pill, the tab bar's heart (22, 1.6 stroke): outline at 45% when not liked, filled at full strength when liked, with a quick bounce. Tapping when liked removes the like; otherwise it opens the folder picker.
- **Search pill**: capsule in `homePill`, 14 regular text, placeholder in `homeSubtitle`, Lenny mark at the trailing end.
- **Tab bar**: floating capsule, three line icons (home, heart, person), 22 / 1.6 stroke. Active icon at full strength, others at 45%. **Home stays mounted under the other tabs** (hidden, not rebuilt), so coming back to it is instant and its scroll position is kept; Likes and Profile are built when opened. Photos (`CachedAsyncImage`) are decoded once, off the main thread and already shrunk to 1600 px on the long side, so scrolling never waits on a decode.
- **Field pill** (onboarding): same capsule, 14 regular, caret in `homeText`.
- **Small action pills** inside a row ("Add" a friend, accept a request): the highlighted pill, ground and ink swapped (`homeText` fill, `homeBackground` label), 38-40 tall; the quiet counterpart (decline, "Cancel") is the flat `homePill` with the label in `homeText` or `appBadgeRed`. The pencil on a profile photo is the flat round pill too. A search field is a `homePill` capsule.
- **Avatars** are flat: round, no ring, no shadow.
- Placing a new button: reuse one of these. Do not add glass or shadows.

## Patterns

- **Home shelves** (below "Available tonight", `HomeSection`): more ways in than the search bar, in **different layouts so the page has a rhythm** (never two alike side by side). Every shelf has the same header (16 semiBold title, 13 `homeSubtitle` "See all", which opens that shelf's pods in a grid). Three layouts: **hero**, the big portrait cards of "Available tonight" (Room for groups, the kinds Refuges / Cabins; the subtitle line says beds); **tall** (`HomeTallCard`, Highest): 156 x 236 photos, radius 32, the altitude as a big number (30 semiBold, white, \"2734 m\") bottom-left on the flat scrim, name 15 semiBold and region under it; **mosaic** (`HomeMosaic`, a region, \"In Dolomites\"): one lead photo (57% of the width, 270 high, radius 32) and two small ones stacked (radius 26) with 10 between, or a single tall one when the region has two pods, names white on the scrim, the lead also saying its beds. Order: tall, mosaic, hero, **three shelves at most** besides Hand-picked (`HomeSection.maxShelves`): Highest, the busiest region, Room for groups; a kind (Refuges / Cabins) only fills in when one of those is missing, so Home stays short and the search and Lenny do the rest. A shelf needs enough pods (3 for Highest, with an altitude under 6,000 m, and for a kind; 2 for groups and for a region's mosaic). A pod can be on several shelves and a card zooms open from the shelf it was tapped on. Typing in the search bar hides the shelves. (\"Your likes\" was tried and dropped: not useful.) **Only well-documented huts get a shelf** (`PodQuality`: a photo 3, a way to reach it 2, altitude, beds, facilities, a description, hours or a price 1 each, a hand-picked hut +5; 5 or more, or hand-picked), so a shelf never leads with a bare name; the **hand-picked huts** (`pods.featured`, `SQL/featured_huts.sql`) have their own first shelf **Hand-picked** (hero cards, at least 3), and search results are ranked partners first, then by that score, then by name.
- **Search bar, two modes.** Tapping the bar is a plain search (nothing moves; the placeholder is "Search a region"). At the trailing end sits Lenny's mark, **round at rest** (40, a `homeText` disc with `homeBackground` eyes, as in the Paper frame). **When the field gets the keyboard it opens into Ask Lenny**, a solid capsule (118 x 40, label 13 semiBold beside the mark) that stands out from the grey bar, an offer at the moment you're looking for a pod; it shrinks back when you leave the field. Tapping it (or the round mark) enters Lenny mode: the capsule shrinks to the round mark, which rolls one full turn to the leading end and melts into the bar so only the eyes show, the bar inverts (`homeText` fill), the placeholder becomes "Chamonix to Zermatt in 5 days". Leaving the field rolls it back. Tap outside a field to dismiss the keyboard. When a search finds nothing ("No huts match “xyz”") a caption **Ask Lenny to find it ›** (13 `homeSubtitle`, underlined) hands what was typed to Lenny's field.
- **Likes folder** (a folder opened from the Likes tab; Paper `LikesFolderView`). A two-column grid of the folder's pods: tiles 166 x 216 (radius 36, 13 apart, 12 between rows, 24 margins), the pod's name (15 semiBold, white) bottom-left on a soft black scrim. One tile is selected: full strength with a white check circle top-right (26, black 1.6 check); the others sit at 55%. Tap a tile to select it (the first one until you pick). The grid fades into the ground over its lower half (`BottomFade`, the same fade as the Likes tab and the save-to-folder sheet), with the pills on top: back, share link (own folders only), **Open** (opens the selected pod). **Each folder has its own title**: its name at the top (30 semiBold, tracking −0.02em, two lines at most, as the Likes and Home titles, 25 below the safe area) with a quiet 13 `homeSubtitle` line under it (\"4 pods\", or \"Shared by Name\" on a folder shared with you), scrolling with the grid. No tab bar. The same tile is the onboarding's region picker tile. **Press and hold a tile to take a pod out of the folder** (yours only): the tiles wiggle, every tile is at full strength and **the check is swapped for a red minus in the same place** (top-right, 26 round in `appBadgeRed`, a thin white 11 x 1.6 bar); tapping a minus removes that pod from your likes (the tiles close up smoothly, the last one takes you back to Likes); the right-hand pill becomes a highlighted **Done**.
- **Profile** (the Profile tab; no Paper frame yet, proposed from this file). The Likes-style title (30 semiBold, left at the 24 margin), then who you are: a round photo (76) with the name (20 semiBold), "@username" (14, `homeSubtitle`) and "N friends · N likes" (13, `homeSubtitle`). One **Edit profile** text pill (83 tall) under it. Then a flat list, 58 per row, no icons: label 16 regular in `homeText`, a quiet chevron in `homeSubtitle` when the row opens a screen, an optional caption at the trailing end, a hairline in `homeText` at 8%, an activity count as a small `homePill` capsule: Friends, Reservations, Login and security, About Podz. **Reservations' caption is the next trip** (13 `homeSubtitle`: "In 5 days", "Tomorrow", "Today", "Under way"; nothing when no stay is ahead), so the trip is a glance away on the tab. Last, set apart: **Log out** and **Delete account**, labels in `appBadgeRed`, no chevron, delete asks to confirm. Follows the system appearance.
- **Collapsible group** (`CollapsibleHeading`, in `SectionPage.swift`): a `SectionHeading` that folds what's under it, for a group that shouldn't take the page. The same 13 `homeSubtitle` label with the number of rows beside it (60%) and a thin chevron that turns down when open; the whole line is the tap target; it opens and closes in 0.18 s (ease out) with the rows just fading, no sliding; **on the way in they appear one after the other** (0.06 s apart, the first seven), on the way out all together. **Past reservations** use it, **closed by default**, so Upcoming and Lenny's bubble keep the room.
- **About Podz** (Profile > About Podz, `AboutView`; a `SectionPage`, the last row of Profile's list). A 13 `homeSubtitle` line (\"Version 1.0 (1)\"), then three flat rows: **Terms of Service**, **Privacy Policy** (each opens its document in place, `LegalDocumentView`) and **Share anonymous usage** (no chevron; the state is the caption, **On** / **Off**; tapping flips it; it is the switch `Analytics` reads), then a 13 `homeSubtitle` credit line (OpenStreetMap contributors, Wikimedia Commons, \"Not every hut is checked by Podz\"). **Legal documents** are `SectionPage`s too: the title, a 13 `homeSubtitle` \"Last updated\" line, then each part under a `SectionHeading` with its text in 15 regular `homeBodyText` (line spacing 4). The words live in `LegalText`, in English and French. The sign-up screen's e-mail step carries one 13 caption under the field (\"By continuing you accept the Terms and the Privacy Policy\", the two names are links in white) that opens the same document in a sheet (`respectsSafeArea`).
- **Pages Profile opens** (Friends, Reservations, Login and security, About Podz, Edit profile; `SectionPage`). No navigation bar. A 30 semiBold title at the top (same as Profile's), the content below, and at the bottom **the same pill row as the pod detail**: the round back pill (83) plus the page's action as a text pill beside it ("Add friend", "Activity" with a dot when something is new, "Delete" in `appBadgeRed` on a post); pages with no action have only the back pill. The row sits 36 from the screen's bottom edge and the content scrolls behind a fade into the ground; the tab bar steps aside. Content is flat: rows with a hairline (value 16 semiBold, caption 13 `homeSubtitle`), quiet group headings (13 `homeSubtitle`, `SectionHeading`), photo tiles with radius 22-36, no cards or promo banners, no accent colors (a count or dot is `homeText`/`homePill`). Sheets opened from them (add friend, activity) have the pill close button (44) and a 20 semiBold title. **The fade covers the whole screen** (`SectionFade`, `SectionPage(fade:)`): a gradient over the entire page, ignoring the safe areas, from clear at `start` to solid at `solid` (shares of the screen's height). The standard is 0.77 / 0.93; a page with something floating above the pills uses `.tall` (0.62 / 0.78); a page of photos `.photos` (0.71 / 0.91). To move the fade up or down, change those two numbers; nothing shows below it.
- **Trip pass** (`TripPassView`; a stay's page, opened by tapping an upcoming stay in Reservations; the old torn-ticket card is gone). **Built like the pod detail**, the app's signature page: a tall photo (360/852 of the screen) with the big rounded bottom (75), the name and altitude centered under it (20 + 14, as the detail), then the stay in 15 `homeBodyText` (\"Sat 31 Oct → Mon 2 Nov  ·  2 nights  ·  3 guests\") with \"Starts in 5 days\" under it, the **confirmation code** (30 semiBold, tracked; tap to copy) and the Code 128 barcode on a white 300 wide rounded block (white in both modes: dark bars on a light ground are what scan). **Tap anywhere between the photo and the buttons** for the details (the photo shrinks to 200/852): region, address, coordinates, altitude, \"Saved on this phone. It opens without signal.\", the caption **View pod ›** and Apple's own **Add to Apple Wallet** button (required by Apple, the one system control here; hidden until the `generate-trip-pass` edge function is deployed, `TripPassView.offersWallet`); tap again to go back. Control stack as the detail's: back, and **Directions** (highlighted; walking directions in Apple Maps, dimmed without coordinates). The pass is saved on the phone (`TripPassStore`, refreshed at launch only when the fetch worked); with no signal Reservations lists \"Saved on this phone\" instead and the passes still open.
- **Listed huts** (`pods.listing_status`; `SQL/listed_huts.sql`, `tools/import_osm_huts.py`). Two kinds of pod: **partner** huts are reserved in the app; **listed** huts are information only, **booked through the hut itself**. A listed hut's detail has no Book pill: its third pill reads **Book through the hut** (two centered lines; highlighted when the hut has a phone, email or website, dimmed otherwise) and opens a choice of **Call**, **Email** and the **website / booking page**; its details say \"Open: <season>\", \"Listed hut: book through the hut\" and, for OpenStreetMap data, \"Information © OpenStreetMap contributors\" (ODbL requires the credit). On a card the subtitle ends with \"· Listed\". **\"Available tonight\" shows only partner huts**; listed ones are found by searching, by the shelves and by Lenny. Lenny's plan can include them: Reserve books only the partner huts, the row says \"Listed · book through the hut\", Lenny's recap ends with \"X is listed only, so book it through the hut.\", and each listed hut has its own **Book through the hut ›** caption in its row (the quiet quick-reply caption: 13 `homeSubtitle`, underlined, chevron; it opens the same Call / Email / website choices). When every hut is listed there are no questions (\"These huts take their bookings themselves…\") and the highlighted pill is the classic **e-Book** (it opens the same Call / Email / website choices; with several huts it first asks \"Which hut?\"), beside back and the close button. The database refuses a reservation on a listed hut. The import only reads OpenStreetMap and writes a SQL file you run yourself.
- **Classic search reads names like Lenny** (`LennyText.matches`): case, accents and dashes don't matter ("haute savoie" finds "Haute-Savoie"), a name can be typed in part, and one wrong letter is forgiven ("Chamonx", "Haute Savoe"; names under 7 letters must be exact). It looks in the hut's name, country and region.
- **Edit profile** (Profile > Edit profile, `ProfileEditView`; a `SectionPage`). No pop-ups: your photo (96, round) with \"Your photo\" and the caption **Change photo ›** (13 `homeSubtitle`, underlined; tapping the photo or the caption opens the picker), then the four fields written in place: a 13 `homeSubtitle` label over the value in 17 semiBold `homeText` (the username has a grey @), a hairline under each that turns solid (60%) while it has the keyboard. **Press and hold the photo** (here or on Profile) to adjust the current one in `AvatarEditorView`: a full-screen \"Adjust photo\" page (30 semiBold title, 13 `homeSubtitle` hint) with the photo under a round window (at most 330), pinch to zoom (1 to 5x), drag to move (it always covers the window), the captions **Rotate** (a quarter turn) and **Reset** (13 `homeSubtitle`, underlined) under it, and the control stack: back, and **Save** (highlighted), which sends the square under the window (800 px) as the new photo. The return key moves down the fields and saves on the last one. The page's action is **Save** beside back: dimmed until something changed and it's valid (a username is required, the age is a number), then highlighted (inverted); only what changed is sent, then it goes back.
- **Lenny search** (Paper `LennySearch` dark + white; opened by the Search key in the Home pill's Lenny mode, `LennySearchScreen`). A 30 semiBold "Lenny is searching…" title (it becomes "3 huts found" when the search is over, counting different pods), then the route in the inverted pill (60 high, `homeText` fill, `homeBackground` text 16, Lenny's mark at the leading end with its disc melted into the bar), then the steps (`LennyTimeline`): an 18 dot (`homeText` 12% ring, solid dot at full strength when done, 30% while running) on a dotted line, name 15 semiBold (`homeSubtitle` while waiting), caption 13 `homeSubtitle`. All three steps are on screen from the first frame (nothing reflows); the step in progress has a breathing dot and a caption that rotates through little lines of Lenny's ("Asking the marmots for tips…"), a finished step's dot grows to full size while a check draws inside and a soft swell fades out around it once (light haptic); dots are 24, solid, with no outline or ring and a solid line draws down to the next step, the title's dots come and go and Lenny's mark sways in the pill. Reduce Motion keeps the state changes and drops the looping motion. The steps follow the Paper frame's positions (first dot 71 under the pill and 45 from the left; 34 of line between dots (58 dot to dot)), not pinned to the control stack; behind the whole screen is `PlanetLoader`: a huge planet (1.4 screen widths wide) rising from the bottom edge, drawn as rows of tiny `homeText` dots at 7% like the Figma Planet plugin: dots on latitude rows, only the land, each dot's size following the light (upper left) until it vanishes at the shadow edge, turning slowly, still with Reduce Motion, fading out when the search is over. **Errors are Lenny's to tell**: there is no red text on this screen; when a search comes back empty (no place understood, no pods along the route or in the country, nothing loaded) the reply bubble says it in Lenny's words ("I couldn't find any huts along that route. Try another stop, or a wider area.") **the route pill opens again**: after an error it becomes a text field (same inverted pill, the route in it, Search key) so the user can tap, change the route and search again; tapping clears the old route and removes Lenny's error bubble (its face rests) to leave a clean field, and searching restarts the whole screen (steps, planet, Lenny's typing); and Lenny's disc and the bubble turn `appBadgeRed` (white text and mark; a trial), and Lenny's face (`LennyMark`) moves with the mood: a slow sway while it works, a hop when it found something, a shake of the head when it couldn't (Reduce Motion: still); the running step stops breathing and "View selected huts" stays dimmed. Lenny's side of the conversation sits just above the pill row (`LennyReply`): the route in the pill is what you said, and this is the answer, a flat `homePill` bubble (radius 23, 15 regular `homeText`) with Lenny's mark as a 30 avatar; three bouncing dots while it works, then the message types itself out ("I found 3 huts along your route. Want to take a look?"); Reduce Motion drops both. Bottom: the back pill and **View selected huts**: dimmed until every stop is looked up (the last step stays pending until then), then it **highlights** (`DetailTextButton(isHighlighted:)`: inverted, `homeText` fill and `homeBackground` label) with a bounce and a success haptic. No navigation bar, follows the system appearance.
- **What Lenny understands** (`LennyRequestParser`, on the device, any length: the first 2,000 characters are read). Pods, regions and countries are matched by name (case, accents, **dashes, apostrophes and spacing don't matter** ("Haute Savoie" is "Haute-Savoie", "Passo-Santner" is "Passo Santner"), **one typo is forgiven** in names of 7 letters or more ("Lagazuoy", "haute savoye", "Switserland"; shorter names must be exact, too many real words sit one letter apart; the words of a name that was found never become a second place) and French / Italian / German country names don't matter; "Passo Santner" finds "Rifugio Passo Santner"); other places come from Apple's tagger and from capitalised names, in the order said; "Chamonix" alone is a place. It also picks up **how many** ("3 friends", "for 2", "solo", "couple"), **the start** ("next Friday", never "in 5 days") and **the stay** ("5 days", "4 nights", "a week", "weekend"). It also reads **facility words** in English and French (`LennyFacilities`): water / drinking water / eau potable, showers / douche, winter room / salle d'hiver, meals / repas, kitchen, electricity / plug, wifi, heating, toilets / WC, dogs, wheelchair, mapped to the labels pods store. They **rank, they never filter**: at each stop the huts that list everything asked come first, then those with some of it, each group keeping its order (nearest, or most beds), because the data is incomplete and a hut that lists nothing may still have it. Lenny's pick says what it has ("Closest to Cortina, with showers and drinking water") or that none lists it ("Closest to Cortina. None lists showers"), the answer shows it as "with showers" beside the guests and dates, and the summary row adds the matching facilities after the beds. A sentence with facilities and no place ("huts with showers") gets "Tell me where you're heading and I'll look for huts with showers." Facility words in a pod's own name ("Refuge de l'Eau Blanche") are ignored. Stops are placed side by side, pods are searched within 50 km, then 100, then 160, and a long leg (more than 90 km) gets pods "Between A and B". Lenny says what it picked up in its answer ("I found 3 huts along your route (3 guests · from Fri 6 Nov · 7 days)"), the step shows it too, and the booking starts with those answers already made (their chips are on screen, the stay suggests the nights).
- **An area is a route** (`LennyAreaRoute`, on the device). When the sentence is only an area (one country, one region that spreads over 40 km or more, or a known range: Alps, Dolomites, Pyrenees, Mont Blanc, Tyrol, Julian Alps, Tatras), Lenny doesn't give one stop: he plans a **hut-to-hut chain across it**, in walking order. Stops are about a day's walk apart (8-32 km as the crow flies, never doubling back), starting where good huts are thickest; each stop's pick is the best-documented hut there (partner huts first, then the facilities asked for, closed huts left out), with the others within 10 km to swap in. The number of stops is about one per two days of the stay (4 when none is said, 2 to 8). A stop is named after its hut's region (its short name if two share one). With fewer than 6 huts, or no chain to make, the usual search takes over.
- **Route stages and the trace** (`LennyStage`, `LennyRoutePath`, `HikingRouter`). The trace is the **real hiking trail** between each pair of huts, from BRouter (OpenStreetMap paths, the \"hiking-mountain\" profile: marked trails, not roads). Only the two huts' positions are sent; each trail found is kept on the phone (Caches) and simplified to about 30 m; legs over 60 km apart get none. ## Motion

`AppMotion`: quick 0.15, snappy 0.2, standard 0.25. Larger moves use `.smooth`: 0.4 for the search roll, 0.45 for the detail stack / extended. Respect Reduce Motion for anything continuous.

**Reduce Motion, one rule**: keep every change of state (fades, the glow, a mark changing place), drop what loops, spins or bounces. So the delete-mode wiggle (folder tiles, folder bubbles) stops and the minus alone says it; the search pill's mark slides to the leading end without its full turn; a new stay in Reservations glows without the 103% pop. A new animation states its Reduce Motion version in this file.

## Appearance

The whole app follows the system appearance: every screen and sheet is built from the tokens above. Nothing forces light any more (the Profile tab's old `AppearanceState` switch is gone). The one thing that stays white in both modes is the trip pass barcode's paper (`barcodePaper`), so it scans.

**App icon** (`AppIcon.appiconset`, exported from Icon Composer): a black blob with a round hole, on a soft white ground (light), the same blob in white on near-black (dark), and the grey glass version for the tinted mode (`Icon-iOS-ClearDark`, which the system tints). Replace all three together when the logo changes.
