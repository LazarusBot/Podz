# Podz design

The source of truth is the Paper file **Podz** (frames: Onboarding 1-6, Home,
Home2, DetailView, each in dark and white). This file records what those
frames use so the app can follow them without opening Paper.

**Every UI change must follow this file, and must update it when it adds a
color, a button style or a pattern.** Check it before every commit/push.

## Principles

- **UX comes first.** Ease of use beats visual polish and beats adding
  features. Before building, check how many gestures and controls share one
  screen: horizontal paging, vertical swipes, edge swipes, taps on the same
  area and system gestures (swipe-to-dismiss, keyboard dismiss) all compete.
  If a screen is getting crowded or two interactions could fight (the pod
  detail's photo paging vs. swipe-to-dismiss is the example), **tell the
  user before building** and propose the simpler option; remove the
  interaction that is hardest to get right.
- **The traveler app has three tabs: Home, Likes, Profile** (`TravelerTab`). The old Pods tab (friends' posts, the offline Trip Pass) and Posts are gone for now: Posts is a later feature, and the host side keeps its own Mailbox and tabs.
- Pure black / white grounds. **No accent color**: the photos carry all the color.
- Flat. No shadows, no gradients except a fade into the ground and the soft black scrim under a name on a photo tile (white text on photos is the one place text isn't a token). Nothing is glass:
  the Likes folders are flat bubbles (see Patterns).
- Dark and light are both designed. Never hard-code a color: use a token below,
  built with `Color(light:dark:)`.
- Quiet copy: short, lower-key sentences ("Tell him where and when.").

## Shape and spacing

- Content margin 24. Button rows sit 40 from the screen edges on the pod detail, 24 (the grid's edge) on the Likes folder, 56 on onboarding.
- Pill buttons: capsule, **83 tall**, 13 apart. Search pill: 60 tall. Tab bar: 220 x 64.
- Pod cards: 296 x 340, radius 36 continuous, caption 12 below. A peek of the next card (card width = screen - 97).
- Photos that bleed from the top: rounded only along the bottom, radius 75. Onboarding card photo: radius 53, inset 21.
- Lenny mark: 40 round in the search pill; 56 round badge (3pt ground-colored border) on the detail. Photo dots: 10 round, fanned on a 42 radius arc above the badge.
- **Control stack** (the row of pills at the bottom of the pod detail, the Likes folder, the save-to-folder sheet and every page Profile opens: back pill + the page's action): the pills are 83 tall, 13 apart, and the row sits **74 from the screen's physical bottom edge** on every screen, i.e. 40 above the 34pt home-indicator zone (`DetailLayout.controlStackGap` + the inset). One rule: a screen that ignores the bottom safe area (a tab's content, the pod detail) uses `.controlStackPadding()`; a sheet, which already respects it, uses `DetailLayout.controlStackGap` alone. Never hard-code another number. Side margins: 24 on the Likes folder, the save sheet and Profile's pages; 40 on the pod detail.

## Buttons

All buttons are flat capsules in `homePill` (95% opacity) with a 1pt ground-colored border, and use `TapScaleButtonStyle` for the press.

- **Text pill** ("Book", "Open", "Continue", "Touch to start"): 14 regular label in `homeText`.
- **Highlighted pill** (last onboarding step): ground and text swap, `onbHighlight` fill, 14 semiBold label.
- **Back**: 83 x 83 pill, chevron 8 x 12, 1.5 stroke, round caps, `homeText` at 35%.
- **Close** (`CloseButton(style: .pill, size: 44)`): round pill, same fill and 1pt border as back, thin 12pt "x" (1.5 stroke, round caps) in `homeText` at 45%. Only the Likes folder sheet uses it so far; other sheets still use the classic gray circle until they are restyled.
- **Share** (Likes folder): round 83 pill like Back, the app's SF Symbol `link` (16 semibold) in `homeText` at 35%. Not the vector from the Paper example, which wasn't right.
- **Like**: 83 x 83 pill, the tab bar's heart (22, 1.6 stroke): outline at 45% when not liked, filled at full strength when liked, with a quick bounce. Tapping when liked removes the like; otherwise it opens the folder picker.
- **Search pill**: capsule in `homePill`, 14 regular text, placeholder in `homeSubtitle`, Lenny mark at the trailing end.
- **Tab bar**: floating capsule, three line icons (home, heart, person), 22 / 1.6 stroke. Active icon at full strength, others at 45%.
- **Field pill** (onboarding): same capsule, 14 regular, caret in `homeText`.
- **Small action pills** inside a row ("Add" a friend, accept a request): the highlighted pill, ground and ink swapped (`homeText` fill, `homeBackground` label), 38-40 tall; the quiet counterpart (decline, "Cancel") is the flat `homePill` with the label in `homeText` or `appBadgeRed`. The pencil on a profile photo is the flat round pill too. The Add-a-pod form's footer is the highlighted "Next" / "Create pod" pill beside a flat "Back" pill, 56 tall (a plain pill while a step is incomplete). A search field is a `homePill` capsule.
- **Avatars** are flat: round, no ring, no shadow.
- Placing a new button: reuse one of these. Do not add glass or shadows.

## Patterns

- **Home shelves** (below "Available tonight", `HomeSection`): more ways in than the search bar, in **different layouts so the page has a rhythm** (never two alike side by side). Every shelf has the same header (16 semiBold title, 13 `homeSubtitle` "See all", which opens that shelf's pods in a grid). Three layouts: **hero**, the big portrait cards of "Available tonight" (Room for groups, the kinds Refuges / Cabins; the subtitle line says beds); **tall** (`HomeTallCard`, Highest): 156 x 236 photos, radius 32, the altitude as a big number (30 semiBold, white, \"2734 m\") bottom-left on the flat scrim, name 15 semiBold and region under it; **mosaic** (`HomeMosaic`, a region, \"In Dolomites\"): one lead photo (57% of the width, 270 high, radius 32) and two small ones stacked (radius 26) with 10 between, or a single tall one when the region has two pods, names white on the scrim, the lead also saying its beds. Order: tall, mosaic, hero, mosaic, hero, mosaic (at most six). A shelf needs enough pods (3 for Highest, with an altitude under 6,000 m, and for a kind; 2 for groups and for a region's mosaic). A pod can be on several shelves and a card zooms open from the shelf it was tapped on. Typing in the search bar hides the shelves. (\"Your likes\" was tried and dropped: not useful.)
- **Search bar, two modes.** Tapping the bar is a plain search (nothing moves; the placeholder is "Search a region"). At the trailing end sits Lenny's mark, **round at rest** (40, a `homeText` disc with `homeBackground` eyes and mouth, as in the Paper frame). **When the field gets the keyboard it opens into Ask Lenny**, a solid capsule (118 x 40, label 13 semiBold beside the mark) that stands out from the grey bar, an offer at the moment you're looking for a pod; it shrinks back when you leave the field. Tapping it (or the round mark) enters Lenny mode: the capsule shrinks to the round mark, which rolls one full turn to the leading end and melts into the bar so only the eyes and mouth show, the bar inverts (`homeText` fill), the placeholder becomes "Chamonix to Zermatt in 5 days". Leaving the field rolls it back. Tap outside a field to dismiss the keyboard. When a search finds nothing ("No pods match “xyz”") a caption **Ask Lenny to find it ›** (13 `homeSubtitle`, underlined) hands what was typed to Lenny's field.
- **Pod detail.** *Stack*: photo 524/852 of the screen height, name + altitude, two-line teaser, back / like / Book. *Extended* (tap anywhere between the photo and the buttons): photo shrinks to 250/852, full description and facts appear; tap the same area to return. The only swipe on this screen is paging through the photos, and the only way out is the back button (swipe-to-dismiss is off).
- **Likes folder** (a folder opened from the Likes tab; Paper `LikesFolderView`). A two-column grid of the folder's pods: tiles 166 x 216 (radius 36, 13 apart, 12 between rows, 24 margins), the pod's name (15 semiBold, white) bottom-left on a soft black scrim. One tile is selected: full strength with a white check circle top-right (26, black 1.6 check); the others sit at 55%. Tap a tile to select it (the first one until you pick). The grid fades into the ground over its lower half (`BottomFade`, the same fade as the Likes tab and the save-to-folder sheet), with the pills on top: back, share link (own folders only), **Open** (opens the selected pod). No title, no tab bar. The same tile is the onboarding's region picker tile.
- **Profile** (the Profile tab; no Paper frame yet, proposed from this file). The Likes-style title (30 semiBold, left at the 24 margin), then who you are: a round photo (76) with the name (20 semiBold), "@username" (14, `homeSubtitle`) and "N friends · N likes" (13, `homeSubtitle`). One **Edit profile** text pill (83 tall) under it. Then a flat list, 58 per row, no icons: label 16 regular in `homeText`, a quiet chevron in `homeSubtitle` when the row opens a screen, an optional caption at the trailing end, a hairline in `homeText` at 8%, an activity count as a small `homePill` capsule: Friends, Reservations, the hosting row, Login and security, Accessibility. (The hosting row sits before Login and security. While `FeatureFlags.hosting` is false it reads "Switch to host mode", **greyed** (45%), with the caption "Coming soon" at its trailing end (13 `homeSubtitle`) and no chevron, and it can't be tapped. When hosting is on it becomes "Become a host" or "Switch to host mode" / "Switch to traveler mode".) Last, set apart: **Log out** and **Delete account**, labels in `appBadgeRed`, no chevron, delete asks to confirm. Follows the system appearance.
- **Pages Profile opens** (Friends, Reservations, Login and security, Accessibility, Edit profile; `SectionPage`). No navigation bar. A 30 semiBold title at the top (same as Profile's), the content below, and at the bottom **the same pill row as the pod detail**: the round back pill (83) plus the page's action as a text pill beside it ("Add friend", "Activity" with a dot when something is new, "Delete" in `appBadgeRed` on a post, "Open Settings"); pages with no action have only the back pill. The row sits 74 from the screen's bottom edge (40 above the home-indicator zone) and the content scrolls behind a fade into the ground; the tab bar steps aside. Content is flat: rows with a hairline (value 16 semiBold, caption 13 `homeSubtitle`), quiet group headings (13 `homeSubtitle`, `SectionHeading`), photo tiles with radius 22-36, no cards or promo banners, no accent colors (a count or dot is `homeText`/`homePill`). Sheets opened from them (add friend, activity) have the pill close button (44) and a 20 semiBold title. The Add-a-pod form is the exception: it keeps its old look and forces light.
- **Trip pass** (`TripPassView`; a stay's page, opened by tapping an upcoming stay in Reservations; the old torn-ticket card is gone). **Built like the pod detail**, the app's signature page: a tall photo (360/852 of the screen) with the big rounded bottom (75), the name and altitude centered under it (20 + 14, as the detail), then the stay in 15 `homeBodyText` (\"Sat 31 Oct → Mon 2 Nov  ·  2 nights  ·  3 guests\") with \"Starts in 5 days\" under it, the **confirmation code** (30 semiBold, tracked; tap to copy) and the Code 128 barcode on a white 300 wide rounded block (white in both modes: dark bars on a light ground are what scan). **Tap anywhere between the photo and the buttons** for the details (the photo shrinks to 200/852): region, address, coordinates, altitude, \"Saved on this phone. It opens without signal.\", the caption **View pod ›** and Apple's own **Add to Apple Wallet** button (required by Apple, the one system control here); tap again to go back. Control stack as the detail's: back, and **Directions** (highlighted; walking directions in Apple Maps, dimmed without coordinates). The pass is saved on the phone (`TripPassStore`, refreshed at launch only when the fetch worked); with no signal Reservations lists \"Saved on this phone\" instead and the passes still open.
- **Listed huts** (`pods.listing_status`; `SQL/listed_huts.sql`, `tools/import_osm_huts.py`). Two kinds of pod: **partner** huts are reserved in the app; **listed** huts are information only, **booked through the hut itself**. A listed hut's detail has no Book pill: its third pill reads **Book through the hut** (two centered lines; highlighted when the hut has a phone, email or website, dimmed otherwise) and opens a choice of **Call**, **Email** and the **website / booking page**; its details say \"Open: <season>\", \"Listed hut: book through the hut\" and, for OpenStreetMap data, \"Information © OpenStreetMap contributors\" (ODbL requires the credit). On a card the subtitle ends with \"· Listed\". **\"Available tonight\" shows only partner huts**; listed ones are found by searching, by the shelves and by Lenny. Lenny's plan can include them: Reserve books only the partner huts, the row says \"Listed · book through the hut\", Lenny's recap ends with \"X is listed only, so book it through the hut.\", and when every hut is listed there are no questions (\"These huts take their bookings themselves…\") and the pill is **Back to plan**. The database refuses a reservation on a listed hut. The import only reads OpenStreetMap and writes a SQL file you run yourself.
- **Edit profile** (Profile > Edit profile, `ProfileEditView`; a `SectionPage`). No pop-ups: your photo (96, round) with \"Your photo\" and the caption **Change photo ›** (13 `homeSubtitle`, underlined; tapping the photo or the caption opens the picker), then the four fields written in place: a 13 `homeSubtitle` label over the value in 17 semiBold `homeText` (the username has a grey @), a hairline under each that turns solid (60%) while it has the keyboard. **Press and hold the photo** (here or on Profile) to adjust the current one in `AvatarEditorView`: a full-screen \"Adjust photo\" page (30 semiBold title, 13 `homeSubtitle` hint) with the photo under a round window (at most 330), pinch to zoom (1 to 5x), drag to move (it always covers the window), the captions **Rotate** (a quarter turn) and **Reset** (13 `homeSubtitle`, underlined) under it, and the control stack: back, and **Save** (highlighted), which sends the square under the window (800 px) as the new photo. The return key moves down the fields and saves on the last one. The page's action is **Save** beside back: dimmed until something changed and it's valid (a username is required, the age is a number), then highlighted (inverted); only what changed is sent, then it goes back.
- **Lenny search** (Paper `LennySearch` dark + white; opened by the Search key in the Home pill's Lenny mode, `LennySearchScreen`). A 30 semiBold "Lenny is searching…" title (it becomes "3 pods found" when the search is over, counting different pods), then the route in the inverted pill (60 high, `homeText` fill, `homeBackground` text 16, Lenny's mark at the leading end with its disc melted into the bar), then the steps (`LennyTimeline`): an 18 dot (`homeText` 12% ring, solid dot at full strength when done, 30% while running) on a dotted line, name 15 semiBold (`homeSubtitle` while waiting), caption 13 `homeSubtitle`. All three steps are on screen from the first frame (nothing reflows); the step in progress has a breathing dot and a caption that rotates through little lines of Lenny's ("Asking the marmots for tips…"), a finished step's dot grows to full size while a check draws inside and a soft swell fades out around it once (light haptic); dots are 24, solid, with no outline or ring and a solid line draws down to the next step, the title's dots come and go and Lenny's mark sways in the pill. Reduce Motion keeps the state changes and drops the looping motion. The steps follow the Paper frame's positions (first dot 71 under the pill and 45 from the left; 34 of line between dots (58 dot to dot)), not pinned to the control stack; behind the whole screen is `PlanetLoader`: a huge planet (1.4 screen widths wide) rising from the bottom edge, drawn as rows of tiny `homeText` dots at 7% like the Figma Planet plugin: dots on latitude rows, only the land, each dot's size following the light (upper left) until it vanishes at the shadow edge, turning slowly, still with Reduce Motion, fading out when the search is over. **Errors are Lenny's to tell**: there is no red text on this screen; when a search comes back empty (no place understood, no pods along the route or in the country, nothing loaded) the reply bubble says it in Lenny's words ("I couldn't find any pods along that route. Try another stop, or a wider area.") **the route pill opens again**: after an error it becomes a text field (same inverted pill, the route in it, Search key) so the user can tap, change the route and search again; tapping clears the old route and removes Lenny's error bubble (its face rests) to leave a clean field, and searching restarts the whole screen (steps, planet, Lenny's typing); and Lenny's disc and the bubble turn `appBadgeRed` (white text and mark; a trial), and Lenny's face (`LennyMark`) moves with the mood: a slow sway while it works, a hop when it found something, a shake of the head when it couldn't (Reduce Motion: still); the running step stops breathing and "View selected pods" stays dimmed. Lenny's side of the conversation sits just above the pill row (`LennyReply`): the route in the pill is what you said, and this is the answer, a flat `homePill` bubble (radius 23, 15 regular `homeText`) with Lenny's mark as a 30 avatar; three bouncing dots while it works, then the message types itself out ("I found 3 pods along your route. Want to take a look?"); Reduce Motion drops both. Bottom: the back pill and **View selected pods**: dimmed until every stop is looked up (the last step stays pending until then), then it **highlights** (`DetailTextButton(isHighlighted:)`: inverted, `homeText` fill and `homeBackground` label) with a bounce and a success haptic. No navigation bar, follows the system appearance.
- **What Lenny understands** (`LennyRequestParser`, on the device, any length: the first 2,000 characters are read). Pods, regions and countries are matched by name (case, accents and French / Italian / German country names don't matter; "Passo Santner" finds "Rifugio Passo Santner"); other places come from Apple's tagger and from capitalised names, in the order said; "Chamonix" alone is a place. It also picks up **how many** ("3 friends", "for 2", "solo", "couple"), **the start** ("next Friday", never "in 5 days") and **the stay** ("5 days", "4 nights", "a week", "weekend"). Stops are placed side by side, pods are searched within 50 km, then 100, then 160, and a long leg (more than 90 km) gets pods "Between A and B". Lenny says what it picked up in its answer ("I found 3 pods along your route (3 guests · from Fri 6 Nov · 7 days)"), the step shows it too, and the booking starts with those answers already made (their chips are on screen, the stay suggests the nights).
- **Lenny plan** (Paper `LennyPlan` dark + white; `LennyPlanView`). "Check your plan" (30 semiBold) with a 13 `homeSubtitle` line under it ("Lake Braies · 2 of 3"), then the stop's pods in the Likes-folder grid (`FolderPodTile`: picked one full strength with a check, the rest at 55%; tap to pick, tap the picked one again to open it). Under that, one line (20 high, always reserved) says why the picked pod is there: the pod Lenny led with shows Lenny's mark (20 disc, `homeText`), **Lenny's pick** (13 semiBold, `homeText`) and the reason in `homeSubtitle`; the tile itself is the plain folder tile (white check), the pick is told by the line above ("Closest to Lake Braies"; for a country, "Most beds in France, 40"); any other pod just shows how far it is ("3.2 km from Lake Braies"). Pods are ordered closest first. Bottom: the back pill (previous stop, or back to the search) and a text pill "Next stop: <name>", "See summary" on the last stop.
- **Reservations** (Profile > Reservations, `MyReservationsView`; a `SectionPage`). Stays booked one after the other, for the same guests and created together (what Lenny's plan books), are **one trip** (`ReservationTrip`): a 22 Lenny disc with **Planned with Lenny** (13 semiBold), the range, nights and guests in 13 `homeSubtitle` (\"Sat 31 Oct → Wed 4 Nov  ·  4 nights  ·  2 guests\"), then the stays on the same numbered dotted line as Lenny's summary (a 64 photo radius 22, the pod 16 semiBold, \"Sat 31 Oct → Mon 2 Nov  ·  2 nights\" 13 `homeSubtitle`), and one red caption **Cancel trip** (13 regular `appBadgeRed`, a caption not a pill; the alert says it cancels all the stays). Tap a stay to open its pod. **Past** stays are the same rows at 60%, newest first, with \"Cancelled\" in red when it was. **Lenny talks about the next trip** at the bottom of the page, just above the pill row (the list scrolls behind it): a `LennyReply` bubble (its avatar, `homePill` bubble, the message typing itself out) with one tip at a time, and under it the small caption **Another tip ›** (13 `homeSubtitle`, underlined, aligned with the bubble's text) to hear the next one. The empty page is Lenny's invitation: a 40 Lenny disc, **No trips yet** (20 semiBold) and one line telling the user to tell Lenny where and for how long.
- **Lenny summary** (`LennySummaryView`, no Paper frame yet: built from the Profile pages recipe and the search's timeline). "Your plan" (30 semiBold) with a 13 `homeSubtitle` line ("3 stops, one pod each"), then one block per stop in route order along a numbered line (24 `homeText` dot with a 12 semiBold number, the same dotted line as the search's steps): the stop in 13 `homeSubtitle`, then an 88 photo (radius 28) with the pod's name (17 semiBold), "3.2 km away · 24 beds" (13 `homeSubtitle`) and, for Lenny's own pick, its mark (18 disc) with "Lenny's pick" (12 semiBold). The stops settle in one after another (0.08 apart; Reduce Motion: all at once). Tap a block to open the pod. **It books the plan, one question at a time in the control stack**, with Lenny asking in its bubble above (`LennyReply`). Each question is first a plain pill beside the back pill (**Add guest**, then **Add date**, then **Add nights**; they read "2 guests" / "Sat 31 Oct" / "2 nights each" once answered). Tapping it opens the **picker across the whole stack**: the back pill moves away and one big `homePill` pill (83 high) takes its place, holding a thin **−** and a thin **+** (12 x 12 vectors, 1.5 round stroke, like the back chevron) on 75 circles in the classic pill grey with a 0.5pt edge (`homeText` at 25%), 4 in from the pill's edge all round, and the answer between them (guests: the number 24 semiBold over "guests" 12 `homeSubtitle`; date: "31 Oct" 16 semiBold over the weekday; tap the date for a calendar sheet with a highlighted Done); beside it **continue**, the back pill's twin with its chevron turned around, inverted (`homeText` fill, `homeBackground` chevron) because it's the one to press. Does that work?\"; 1 when the route gives no duration). Each stop starts where the one before ends; once the start and the nights are answered the captions show the range (\"Lake Braies  ·  Mon 2 Nov → Wed 4 Nov\"). **Every decision shows on the screen as a chip** (flat `homePill` capsule, 38 high, 14 regular: \"3 guests\", \"Sat 31 Oct\", \"2 nights each\") in a row under the title, appearing as each is answered; **tap a chip to change that answer on its own** (the picker opens for it and Continue goes back to the review). The chips lock once it's booked. Then the pill becomes **Reserve** (highlighted, inverted); Lenny recaps ("3 nights for 2 guests, from Fri 31 Oct. Shall I book it?"). Back steps back through the questions (from the first it returns to the plan); the pills come back for each next question. While it books Lenny's bubble types and the pill reads "Reserving…"; booked: Lenny hops and says "Booked! 3 nights for 2 guests, all set." and the pill becomes **View reservations** (opens Profile > Reservations through `TabRouter`); a stop that can't be booked: Lenny's red bubble says which one ("I booked 1 of 3. I couldn't book X for 2 Nov. It may be full, so try other dates.") and Reserve only retries what's left. Fade into the ground under the pills (`BottomFade`).
- **Folder bubbles = a foam** (Paper `LikesFolderSheet`, dark + white; used on the Likes tab and in the save-to-folder sheet; the word stays "folder"). A folder is a flat shape in `homePill`, 300 across when it's the only one, with its name (15 semiBold, `homeText`) in the middle and up to two of its pods' photos tucked inside its edge (119 x 141 at full size, radius 30, at 55%, cut off by the bubble's edge, mirrored on alternate bubbles). Bubbles are **packed tight, like soap foam**, not spread out: each sinks a little into its neighbors and the two **flatten against each other** along a straight edge with a 6pt gap and softly rounded corners (`FoamLayout` / `FoamCellView`). The cluster sits in the middle column and may be cropped a little by the screen edges. **They shrink as folders are added** (base radius 100% for one folder, 88% for two, 76% for three, 63% from four) and **each bubble's size follows the number of pods inside it**: its area is proportional to the count compared with the average, so a folder with four times the pods is twice as wide, equal folders are equal, an empty one is small (never below 55% of the base) and none is wider than the screen allows (the biggest is capped at 160% of the base). **Pinch out to unpack, pinch in to pack**: the bubbles follow the fingers, drift apart into plain circles (no two touching) and are packed again by pinching in; on release it settles with a soft spring to whichever side you passed halfway (never snapping back), and the flat edges grow and fade smoothly along the way. It's a two-finger gesture, so it doesn't compete with scrolling, taps or long-press. ## Motion

`AppMotion`: quick 0.15, snappy 0.2, standard 0.25. Larger moves use `.smooth`: 0.4 for the search roll, 0.45 for the detail stack / extended. Respect Reduce Motion for anything continuous.

## Appearance

Onboarding, Home, the Likes tab (overview and folder view), the pod detail and Profile with the pages it opens follow the system appearance. The folder picker and share-folder sheets follow it too. Everything else (the host shell, the Book sheet, the Add-a-pod form) has no dark palette yet and forces light with `.preferredColorScheme(.light)`.
